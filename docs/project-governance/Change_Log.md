## Change-control procedure

1. Raise the proposed change in Jira or at a team meeting.
2. Review the effect on scope, schedule, quality, responsibilities and deliverables.
3. Assign an owner and update the relevant Jira issue.
4. Record material accepted changes in this log.
5. Upload evidence to GitHub and review it before merging into `main`.
